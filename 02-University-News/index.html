University Celebrates Annual Technology Festival

Bangladesh University of Professionals has successfully organized its annual technology festival. Students from different departments participated in programming contests, robotics competitions and project showcases.

The event was attended by teachers, students and guests. Several innovative student projects were presented during the festival.

The university authority congratulated all participants for their outstanding performance and contribution.
